# Incomplete Work

List everything you did not finish, and why. A task you failed but diagnosed
correctly earns up to 60% of its marks. A task you skipped silently earns 0 —
so list every skip here, even ones that feel embarrassing.

Per entry: task number, what you tried, where it broke, what you'd do with
more time.
